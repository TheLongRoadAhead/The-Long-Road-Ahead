The Synopsis

A deadly plague has overrun the United States in 2024 turning the population into flesh-eating zombies. 2 years later After receiving a broken, distorted satellite message from their father, two brothers must brave a 4,400km cross-country journey from the flooded streets of Miami all the way to a refugee camp in Los Angeles to find him.
